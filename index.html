Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Exercise Moose is free and open source under the MIT license.

Does it send my data anywhere?

No. No accounts, no analytics, no network calls. Your streak, settings and avatar stay on your Mac.

Do I need Claude Code?

No. The 30-minute breaks, streaks and everything else work on their own. Claude Code just adds agent breaks and usage in the menu.

Which Macs does it run on?

Any Mac with macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later.

Can I use my own avatar?

That's the idea. Follow the avatar guide on GitHub to turn your photo into a character and animate it, or keep the sample.

Is this medical advice?

No. These are gentle desk movements. Stop if anything hurts, and ask a doctor or physiotherapist if you have an injury or condition.
